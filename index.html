About Pirate Isles

Pirate Isles is a cheerful top-down sailing adventure. You captain a small sloop around a bright island sea, deliver cargo, dig up treasure and chase off pirate ships.

There are 12 story missions, four ports and eight islands, port contracts, and a Shipyard with upgrades and bigger ships. The story ends with Captain Redbeard and Admiral Blackfin.

3 quick tips

  1. Drag anywhere and the ship sails towards your finger. Drag further to sail faster.
  2. Your cannons aim themselves, so circle pirates instead of charging them.
  3. Sail away from battle to repair. Your hull slowly heals when you are out of combat.

How to play

⛵ Drag anywhere to steer: your ship sails toward your finger. Drag further to go faster. Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys.

💥 Your cannons fire by themselves at nearby pirate ships. Stay close enough and keep moving to dodge their cannonballs.

⚓ Sail slowly into a port and tap Dock to pick missions, repair and upgrade your ship.

🗺️ Follow the yellow arrow to your mission: deliver cargo, dig up buried X treasure, collect floating chests and chase off pirates.

👑 Earn gold and rank, beat the pirate bosses and buy bigger ships: Sloop, Brigantine and Galleon!
