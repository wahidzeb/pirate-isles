How to play

⛵ Drag anywhere to steer: your ship sails toward your finger. Drag further to go faster. Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys.

💥 Your cannons fire by themselves at nearby pirate ships. Stay close enough and keep moving to dodge their cannonballs.

⚓ Sail slowly into a port and tap Dock to pick missions, repair and upgrade your ship.

🗺️ Follow the yellow arrow to your mission: deliver cargo, dig up buried X treasure, collect floating chests and chase off pirates.

👑 Earn gold and rank, beat the pirate bosses and buy bigger ships: Sloop, Brigantine and Galleon!
